First release. One app for both Apple silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 14 or later).

- Import your resume (PDF, Word or text) into an editable master resume that never changes when you tailor.
- Add jobs by link or by pasting the description. Job Bot reads responsibilities, required and preferred qualifications, keywords, location and pay.
- Compare each job with your experience: evidence for every requirement, gaps, and follow-up questions before anything changes.
- Tailored resumes with reviewable suggestions, each showing why it may help and what supports it.
- Cover letters and application answers drafted only from your profile.
- Three templates (ATS Simple, Modern, Classic); export to PDF, Word and plain text.
- Version history with compare and restore.
- Application tracking by status.
- Find Jobs: search terms, location filters (Remote, Atlanta, New York), job sites opened in your browser, and public company job boards.
- Pivot paths, credentials with sources, and pay comparison.
- Privacy controls: local storage, Keychain for keys, a log of everything sent, export and delete.
- In-app updates from GitHub Releases.
