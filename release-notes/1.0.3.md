- Jobs now has two lists. Found shows postings from your connected company job boards, already read into responsibilities, qualifications and pay, with the ones your master resume covers best at the top. Saved holds the jobs you're working on.
- Job Bot checks the boards when you open Jobs and the last check is more than six hours old, or when you click Check for New Listings. New listings are marked with a dot and counted in the sidebar.
- Each listing shows how many required qualifications have evidence in your master resume, what has none yet, and the posting's keywords. Save it to compare it in full, tailor your resume and write a cover letter, or mark it Not Interested so it doesn't come back.
- More companies can be read: Workday careers sites (Curriculum Associates, Scholastic, Kaplan, College Board, TNTP and Amplify), SmartRecruiters (Renaissance, Illuminate) and Workable (Riverside Insights). They're connected automatically from the careers links you already have. If a site doesn't allow reading, Search details in Find Jobs says so and the careers page still opens in your browser.
- LinkedIn, Indeed and Handshake still open in your browser, because they don't allow apps to read their listings.
